# Open Questions for Client

1. **Domain Name:** Once the production domain is acquired, supply it to populate `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_DOMAIN` and configure `info@<domain>` email routing.
2. **Service Copy Confirmation:** Review and finalize the text descriptions of the four confirmed services (Mechanical, Oil & Gas Services, Facility Management, Electrical & Telecommunications).
3. **Corporate Details:** Year of incorporation, CAC RC Registration Number, and regulatory certifications (COREN, DPR/NUPRC, NCEC).
4. **Statistics:** Official operational metrics (e.g. project completion rates, client retention, safety man-hours) to activate the statistics counters.
5. **Real Photography & Client Logos:** Real site photography from Warri/Niger Delta operations and approved partner/client logos to activate the testimonials and partner bands.
6. **Social Media & WhatsApp:** Corporate handles for LinkedIn, Facebook, and official WhatsApp hotline.
