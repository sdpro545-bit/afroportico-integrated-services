# Creative Additions Log

Consistent with Section 15.1 of the Master Build Prompt, creative refinements must reinforce the brand identity without altering layout integrity:

1. **Brand Arch Geometry in Cutout Cards:**
   - The triangular arch apex and curved portal from the Afroportico emblem are reflected in the inverted-corner notched tab design on feature cards and image framing.
   - This geometric resonance ties the UI component shapes directly to the corporate logo.

2. **Subtle Gradient Line Separators:**
   - In accordance with the reference's hairline with terminal dot, cards feature a clean hairline terminating in a discrete brand turquoise dot (`--teal-500`).

3. **Logo Protective Tile System:**
   - On dark surfaces (Deep Navy `#04374D` sections, footer, mobile drawer, preloader), the unaltered Afroportico emblem is enclosed within a crisp white rounded tile with 16px radius and 12px padding, ensuring the dark navy half retains its bold contrast and visual authority.
